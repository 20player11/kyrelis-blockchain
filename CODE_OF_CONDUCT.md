# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want KYRELIS spaces to be welcoming, constructive and accessible regardless of experience, background, identity or skill level. Participants agree to engage professionally and without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and open to differing technical views.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while honoring boundaries.
- Keep debates focused on code, evidence, safety and project goals.
- Protect other contributors' privacy and confidential security reports.
- Credit original work and comply with applicable licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, impersonation, spamming, or publication of another person's credentials or private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner `@20player11` through an available private GitHub contact channel. Do not use public issues for personal or sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, warn participants, restrict access, or ban repeat/serious offenders. Reports will be evaluated with reasonable care; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed beyond the capabilities of the reporting channel.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review comments and affiliated community spaces. It is inspired by the principles of the Contributor Covenant, with wording tailored to this repository.
